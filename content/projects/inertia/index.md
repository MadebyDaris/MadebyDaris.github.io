## Overview

**Inertia** is a custom-built physics engine designed to handle complex simulations ranging from general macroscopic physics to astronomy and robust quantum mechanics.

## Development Journey

*   **July 2025 (Initial Overview):** The engine started with a basic OpenGL (Ogl) rendering pipeline, establishing the core loop and general macroscopic physics solver.
*   **August 2025 (Enhanced UI):** Focused on overhauling the user interface to make parameter tweaking and visualization much more intuitive for complex simulations.
*   **November 2025 (Quantum Mechanics):** The engine was significantly expanded to include robust quantum mechanics simulations, allowing for real-time visualization of wavefunctions and quantum phenomena alongside standard classical mechanics.
